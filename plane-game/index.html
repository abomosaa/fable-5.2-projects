Gamepad

Left stick: pitch & roll · right stick: rudder / throttle · triggers: throttle · A: brakes · B: gear · X / Y: flaps · D-pad: trim, spoilers, reverse.

First flight

Release the parking brake (B), hold R for full power, keep straight with Q/E, and ease back on S at rotation speed. To land: flaps down, fly the PAPI lights (two red, two white) at approach speed, close the throttle over the threshold and flare.
