# Asset manifest

Every third-party asset bundled with or used to build Range Day: author, source, licence, local path.
Machine-readable originals with download URLs and checksums: `docs/manifest/*.json`. Raw downloads live in
`assets_src/` (not committed); the processed, game-ready files are in `game/assets/`.

Licence summary: CC0 (Poly Haven, ambientCG, Free Firearm Sound Library, most Freesound items), CC BY (first-person arms rig,
a few Freesound items, OpenAIR impulse response), SIL OFL (font), MIT / Apache-2.0 (engine and audio middleware), and the
Sonniss #GameAudioGDC royalty-free licence (use embedded in a game permitted; **the raw library files may not be redistributed** —
the game ships edited, layered derivatives only; do not publish `assets_src/audio/sonniss-gdc`).
